Acceptance checklist:
## Public acceptance checklist

### R1 - Loading Screen and Hero Entrance
After the user opens or refreshes the page, a dark, full-screen loading screen first displays “Gsap.” Its letters enter in sequence from different directions—from the left and right and from above and below—then exit downward one letter at a time. Once the overlay fades out, the hero branding, oversized title, collection description, and call to action become readable. After loading finishes, the overlay no longer intercepts wheel or pointer input, and the main video remains muted, plays automatically, and loops. If the media’s first frame has not yet arrived, the media frame and body-copy position remain reserved. A refresh replays only one set of loading and hero animations for the current page, and repeated refreshes do not stack overlays or duplicate letter tracks.

### R2 - Navigation and Vertical Text Flips
On desktop, as the user moves the pointer over the brand link, sign in, the top start learning now button, the in-page explore collection action, and the footer start learning now button in turn, the two identical text copies within each clipped frame move vertically to replace one another. When the pointer leaves, the first copy returns fully into place; during rapid back-and-forth movement, the track converges on the latest pointer state. Collection, Pricing, FAQ, and the brand link retain their respective destinations. The active hover must not move the scroll position, change any other entry, or leave partial characters visible. The footer’s permissions & terms and privacy policy labels turn fluorescent green on hover and return to their original color when the pointer leaves.

### R3 - Opening-Section Video and Line-by-Line Copy
As the user scrolls down from the hero through the main showcase video and the Improve your animation skills section, the large video moves toward the lower left and shrinks substantially in sync with the same scroll progress, while each line of explanatory copy enters continuously from below its own clipped container. After the paragraph ends, the explore collection action remains fully readable. If the user stops midway or scrolls back up, the video’s position and scale and the revealed amount of every line return to the states corresponding to that scroll position. After the user quickly crosses the opening section, the media does not cover subsequent content, and the video remains muted and looping within its original media frame.

### R4 - Learning Title and Benefit Cards
After the user enters the near-black learning section, “Learn how to use the coolest JS library” is revealed from below in letter groups. The Beginners friendly, Easy to implement, and Performance optimized cards then bounce up from below in staggered groups and settle into a horizontal desktop card group with slight alternating tilts. As scrolling continues, each card remains at its completed position and no title characters remain outside the clipping area. When the user scrolls back up, the entrance sequence reverses according to scroll progress. On narrow screens, the same cards instead form an upright single column, while each card’s copy and graphic remain paired correctly.

### R5 - Large-Title Flips and Agency Logos
As the user scrolls through the Learn it, Tweak it, and Use it oversized titles, two identical copies above and below form a clipped track for each title, and the different letter groups switch upward in sequence. Once the preceding title finishes, the next title enters in page order; reverse scrolling restores the text copy that was visible before the transition. After the titles, trusted by awesome agencies and the agency logos enter the reading sequence, with a wide logo strip on desktop and a compact version on narrow screens. Repeated forward and backward scrolling does not change the order of the three titles or allow the agency graphic to cover the detail introduction.

### R6 - Section Arch Divider
As the user scrolls past the opening section’s explore collection content and approaches the near-black learning section, a horizontal row of black rounded arches rises from below its container. The arches on the left and right sides start first, followed one by one in symmetric order toward the center, gradually transitioning the light content into the dark section below. At the completed endpoint, all arches align into a continuous scalloped edge that joins the black background; when the user scrolls back up, they descend along the same paths in reverse. Whether the user pauses, crosses the interval quickly, or enters it again, the arches’ shape always reflects the current scroll position.

### R7 - Effects Layered Media
When the user enters the Effects content block and scrolls down, the section name, the description of scroll animations, mouse-based interactions, and drag movements, and the Free GSAP Core / No WebGL and Webflow compatible labels appear in reading order. Within the same media frame, the layered example images expand one layer at a time from a scaled-down state to cover the preceding layer according to scroll progress. If the user stops or scrolls back, each layer holds or returns to its corresponding scale; later images retract in sequence to reveal the bottom layer again. The media remains within the square clipping frame and does not cover the Learning content block. The labels and paragraphs remain associated with Effects, and the stated current collection total remains consistent everywhere it appears.

### R8 - Learning Tutorial Content
When the user continues scrolling to the Learning content block, the page presents explanations of step-by-step tutorials, downloadable finished projects, and customization capabilities, and plays the tutorial video in the corresponding large media frame. The video plays automatically, loops, and is muted by default; it uses crop-to-fill within the container without changing the reading order of the adjacent and following paragraphs. When the user returns upward to Effects or repeatedly crosses between the two content blocks, the Learning video retains its own media settings, and the Effects image layers do not remain over the tutorial area. If the remote video becomes ready later, it fills only the existing frame and does not reposition the detail content or the closing question.

### R9 - Closing Question and Footer
When the user leaves the detail content and enters the light closing section, the letters in “So, ready to animate?” split into two groups that enter from above and below while their opacity changes. As scrolling advances, the groups converge into the complete question; during reverse scrolling, each group leaves along its own incoming path. The dark rounded-arch edge below then connects to the footer. Eager to learn GSAP?, the Get ready to animate marquee, start learning now, the policy labels, and the Made With Gsap title in the footer all remain readable. The marquee units loop continuously in the horizontal direction without expanding the page width. After the pointer leaves the button and policy labels, their text and colors return to their initial states.

### R10 - Reverse Scrolling and Re-entry
When the user scrolls continuously down and then back up from the middle of the page, the video scaling, line-by-line text, rounded arches, learning-section title, cards, oversized-title flips, layered images, and closing question all advance or reverse according to the smooth-scrolling page’s current progress and do not continue drifting when the user stops. After the user quickly passes through multiple sections, objects from each section that has been left settle at their corresponding endpoints and do not obscure the next section. Re-entering the same interval does not reuse an old hover target or previous scroll direction. Refreshing or reopening clears all prior-session scrolling and animation state, leaving only one active set for the current page.

### R11 - Narrow-Screen Reflow and Viewport Changes
When the user narrows the viewport to phone width, the desktop navigation links and account action area collapse, and the top navigation switches to get started and a two-line menu button. The hero title and description, upright cards, full-width detail media, vertically arranged question, and footer appear in single-column order, while the agency logos and arch dimensions use their narrow-screen forms. When the viewport returns to desktop width, the complete navigation, horizontally tilted card group, wide agency logo strip, and magazine-style detail layout are restored. Scroll coordinates, media cropping, and text wrapping update for the new size; the current content section remains the same before and after the size change, and no browser-level horizontal scrolling appears.
