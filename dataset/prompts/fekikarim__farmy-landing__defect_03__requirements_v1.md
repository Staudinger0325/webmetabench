Acceptance checklist:
## Public acceptance checklist

### R1 - Brand Loading and Homepage Content Flow
When a user first opens the Farmy Net homepage, a full-screen loading screen must appear first with the brand logo, name, subtitle, loading message, an advancing progress bar, and looping background light blobs. When loading finishes, this screen must fade out as the homepage fades in, followed by one continuous vertical flow in this order: Hero, Growing Together, Features, How It Works, Team, Frequently Asked Questions, Download Farmy Net, and the footer. No blank-content flash may occur at any point in this entrance sequence. After the loading screen has completed its exit, it must no longer cover the navigation or receive clicks, and as the user scrolls through the page, each section heading and card must appear according to its own one-time entrance sequence.

### R2 - Fixed Navigation, Themes, and Mobile Drawer
On desktop, clicking Features, How It Works, Team, FAQ, Download, Get Started, or the brand logo must smoothly scroll the page to the corresponding section or the top of the page. After the user scrolls down, the fixed navigation must change from a transparent state to a floating state with blur and shadow, while clicking the theme button must switch the background, text, cards, borders, and icons together between light and dark color schemes. On narrow screens, only the brand, theme, and menu controls must remain. When the menu opens, a right-side drawer and overlay must appear; clicking a section link or the overlay must close the drawer. Repeated open-and-close cycles must leave no overlay behind, and the page must never develop horizontal scrolling.

### R3 - Hero Motion and Demo Video
While a user remains in the Hero section or scrolls downward, the particles must keep moving and re-enter the frame after crossing an edge, the background light blobs must drift slowly, and the agricultural icons on wide screens must float up and down. The Hero copy must continuously move downward, shrink, and fade out in response to scrolling. Clicking Get Started must take the user to the Download Farmy Net section, while clicking Watch Demo must open a widescreen player over a dark, blurred overlay. Before the player loads, it must show Initializing Player... and a spinning indicator, and the main page must stop scrolling while the modal is open. Clicking the close button or the outer overlay must make the video container complete its scale-and-fade exit and restore main-page scrolling; clicking inside the container must keep the modal open.

### R4 - Statistics and One-Time Count-Up
When a user scrolls to Growing Together for the first time, the Active Users, Farm Lands, Products Sold, and Satisfaction Rate cards must fade in and move upward in sequence, and each number must count continuously from zero to its current target value. When the live data service is available, its returned data must be used; when it is temporarily unavailable, the default data must remain visible immediately while a limited number of recovery attempts run in the background. Once counting finishes, leaving the statistics section, making small upward or downward movements near its boundary, or returning later must leave the completed values intact without resetting or replaying the count. Hovering over a card must change only its position, shadow, and icon pose, and the download count text in the Download Farmy Net section must remain consistent with the current user data.

### R5 - Platform Capabilities, Workflow, and Roles
When a user scrolls into Features and How It Works, the capability cards must appear in sequence and present Farm Management, Marketplace, Digital Wallet, Land & Rental, Multi-Role Platform, AI Assistant, Secure & Verified, Real-time Updates, Gamified Experience, Analytics Dashboard, Delivery Network, and Sustainability Focus. Hovering over the current card must raise it and reveal its corresponding gradient and icon motion, and Start Exploring must take the user to the Download Farmy Net section. The workflow must retain the fixed order Create Your Account, Explore & Connect, Transact Securely, and Grow Together. The Farmers, Investors, Workers, and Customers cards that follow must accurately present their respective business scopes, and responsive changes in column count must not alter the content order or the association between each role and its capabilities.

### R6 - Team Cards and Image States
When a user scrolls to Team, each member card must show the member's name, role, bio, avatar, and only the social or contact links actually provided for that member. While an avatar is loading, it must show a rotating placeholder in that member's color palette; after a successful load, it must show the cropped image, and after a load failure or when the image is missing, it must switch to a gradient block containing the member's initials. Hovering over an avatar must enlarge the image and show the role overlay, then restore the avatar when the pointer leaves. Clicking a GitHub, LinkedIn, portfolio, or email link must open its corresponding external destination, and an unavailable link must occupy no empty slot. The phone preview in the Download Farmy Net section must likewise provide a shimmer loading state, a clear loaded state, a failure message, and a Retry action that returns it to the loading state.

### R7 - Single-Item FAQ Accordion
When a user enters Frequently Asked Questions, the first item must be expanded by default. After the user clicks another item, the current answer must collapse with height and opacity animations while the new answer expands at the same time, and the brand-color outline and arrow direction must identify only the currently expanded item. Clicking the current item must close it, and after rapid consecutive clicks on several items, only the item specified by the final click may remain expanded. At any time, the FAQ list may consume the additional height of at most one answer. Once an old answer collapses, its space must be released, and Still have questions?, Contact Us, the Download Farmy Net section, and the footer must move continuously only with the currently expanded item; they must not be pushed progressively farther down by the history of earlier clicks.

### R8 - Download Status and Platform Links
When a user reaches Download Farmy Net, the section must show the app description, rating, Secure & Private message, download count text based on the current user data, and a continuously floating phone preview. While the data is loading, it must show Syncing... with a spinning icon; when loading finishes, it must show the current value. Online data must include an activity indicator, while fallback data must remain readable without displaying the online indicator. Clicking App Store must open the iOS Coming Soon! modal, and Join Waitlist must open a pre-addressed email. Later, the upper-right close button, and the outer overlay must each close the modal. Clicking Google Play must directly open the currently configured Android installation-package destination.

### R9 - Footer and Legal Document Flow
When a user clicks an internal section link in the footer, the page must smoothly return to the corresponding content. Clicking an external resource, social link, or email link must open the corresponding destination, while clicking Privacy Policy or Terms of Service must open its own separate document. Each legal page must first show a brief skeleton state and then present a return control, title, search box, table of contents, clause text, and a contact or related-document action. As the user scrolls through a document, the desktop sidebar or mobile horizontal table of contents must synchronously mark the current clause, and clicking a table-of-contents entry must reach its target. Search must filter clause titles, descriptions, and body text; a dedicated empty state must appear when nothing matches, and clearing the query must restore all clauses.

### R10 - Responsive Layout and Page Lifecycle
When a user resizes the viewport among desktop, tablet, and phone widths, the card grids, button groups, Team section, Download Farmy Net section, and footer must change columns at their breakpoints or stack vertically. The Hero particle canvas must adopt the new dimensions, decorative objects must not cover the main content, and the fixed navigation, mobile drawer, mobile legal-page table of contents, and full-screen modals must retain the correct stacking order without introducing horizontal overflow. After the user closes a modal, leaves the page, or repeatedly enters a content section, scroll tracking, canvas animation frames, count timers, data polling, and viewport observation must be initialized and cleaned up at the appropriate lifecycle boundaries. If the third-party video or statistics service is temporarily unreachable, all other sections must remain browsable.

### R11 - Consistent Completed State When Statistics Re-enter
After all Growing Together values supplied by the current data source and their corresponding icons have completed their initial increment and drawing sequence, scrolling the entire section out of view and returning must keep the numbers and all icons in the completed state. Unless the user refreshes the page or performs an explicit global reset, returning must not erase any icon in reverse, redraw icons in card order, replay the first-entry sequence, restart a number from its beginning, or leave the numbers and icons at contradictory visible progress. A value may show genuinely refreshed data, but ordinary re-entry must not be treated as a first load.
