Acceptance checklist:
## Public acceptance checklist

### R1 - Long-form Content and In-page Navigation
When the user clicks Manifest, Werte, Zahlen, Tiers, FAQ, Join, or the brand mark from the fixed header, hero buttons, membership cards, or footer, the page must scroll smoothly to the actual corresponding section and always leave visible clearance for the fixed header; repeated jumps and reverse scrolling must not obscure section headings. After the page leaves the top, the header must switch to a paper-cream background, an ink-black divider, and a hard shadow, then return to its hero state when the page returns to the top. Narrower viewports must hide the central navigation while retaining the brand and Join, and every destination must remain reachable without horizontal overflow.

### R2 - Hero Statement Cycle
When the user opens the page and waits, the hero must cycle through three mixed German-English statements in a fixed order. The current statement must enter from a lowered, transparent state while the previous statement exits in sync, and the cycle must return to the starting statement after one complete round; the introduction, credibility summary, and action buttons must remain in place throughout each transition. After fonts finish loading, the window is resized, or the device orientation changes, the title container must readjust to the greatest actual height among the candidate statements so that longer statements do not overlap the content below. With a reduced-motion preference, the hero must present only one clearly readable static statement.

### R3 - Scroll Reveals and Divider Strip
As the user scrolls down from the hero, section eyebrows, headings, body copy, cards, and buttons must reveal in sequence near the viewport from slightly lowered and transparent states, then remain visible after completing their entrance. Scrolling back and then forward again must not replay a completed entrance or accumulate vertical displacement or opacity changes on any element. The black divider strip must move continuously across the page and add subtle vertical parallax while a wide-screen fine-pointer device scrolls past it. Touch devices must disable the parallax, while reduced-motion mode must stop the strip movement but preserve all text, the full divider space, and the original reading order.

### R4 - Brand Content and Data Synchronization
As the user scrolls through Manifest, Zahlen, the joining steps, the member testimonial, Fuer wen, the membership tiers, FAQ, and JETZT in sequence, the sections must present one consistent community narrative with German as the primary language and English as the supporting language. The Deborah Levi testimonial, four member profiles, and each tier name and set of benefits must appear in their corresponding content sections. Current information such as community size, regions, events, member composition, prices, billing periods, available places, and cohort details must come from the same current content across summaries, scoreboards, cards, questions and answers, and stickers, so an update never leaves conflicting stale values.

### R5 - Membership Tier Card States
When the user scrolls to Tiers, the responsive grid must fully display the Athlete, Booster, Business, and Partner cards, including their audience descriptions, current price wording, benefits, and action links. The recommended tier's "most popular" badge must pop from a transparent, scaled-down state to its designated rotation angle the first time it enters the viewport. When a fine pointer enters a card, the card must lift slightly toward the upper left and its hard shadow must grow; it must return to the grid baseline on pointer exit, and the dark Partner card must use a mint-colored shadow. Clicking any tier button must move to the final Join section, and repeated hovering must not accumulate displacement.

### R6 - Sticker Entrance and Idle Drift
When the user scrolls into a sticker cluster in Werte, Fuer wen, or JETZT, the stickers must pop in from scaled-down, transparent states with staggered timing in their current layout order. After the entrance finishes, each sticker must regain its full designed size, original rotation angle, position, and hard shadow before beginning an idle drift limited to tiny vertical movement and angle changes. Odd- and even-positioned stickers must use different rhythms. Hovering a sticker must pause only that sticker, and it must resume from full size when the pointer leaves. Scrolling back, entering again, or waiting for a long time must not reintroduce the entrance scale; with a coarse pointer, a narrow viewport, or a reduced-motion preference, the stickers must remain static and readable.

### R7 - Custom Cursor Motion, Inversion, and Magnetic Response
When a desktop user with a fine pointer moves the mouse, the 6px cursor dot must immediately reach the current pointer position while the 32px outer ring follows the dot along a frame-by-frame interpolated path, retaining a visible gap during continuous movement and gradually converging after movement stops. When the dot and ring lie on opposite sides of the WERTE light section, the ZAHLEN black section, or another light-dark boundary, both must continue to invert according to the background each one covers; the ring outline must not switch to normal compositing, lose opacity, or nearly disappear. On entering links, buttons, or FAQ headings, the dot and ring must enlarge together and switch to the accent color. A magnetic button must shift slightly according to the pointer's direction relative to its center and spring back when the pointer leaves, and rapidly crossing adjacent objects must not leave the previous object's state behind. Touch devices and reduced-motion mode must use the system cursor.

### R8 - FAQ and Keyboard Operation
When the user clicks any FAQ heading, its answer must expand directly below it, the plus sign must rotate into a close shape, and the item must gain a hard shadow. Clicking the same heading again must collapse the answer and restore the symbol and shadow, while opening additional items must not force already open items to close. Keyboard focus must reach the navigation, buttons, tier action links, and every FAQ heading in sequence. Enter or Space on an FAQ heading must produce the same expanded state as a click, and the high-contrast focus outline must remain visible without being clipped by the fixed header, FAQ container, or adjacent content.

### R9 - Calls to Action and Return Paths
When the user clicks a join entry point in the hero, fixed header, membership cards, or JETZT section, the page must move to the dark Join action section while preserving the relationship among the "Join the movement" email action, the "Book 30-min call" action that returns to Manifest, and the bilingual call-to-action copy. The in-page return must continue to use smooth scrolling with clearance for the fixed header. The email entry point must launch an external email action addressed to the brand without changing the current state of the long-form page. If the user cancels the email action and returns, enters Join again, or returns to Manifest, already revealed sections must not become hidden again or jump to an unrelated position.

### R10 - Responsive Content Reflow
As the user resizes the window among wide, medium, and narrow viewports, the two-column narrative, data grid, and membership grid must successively reflow to column counts suited to the current width. Sticker clusters must contract within their own canvases, while nonessential peripheral decorations and the header's central navigation must hide when space requires it; the main content and action links must remain reachable. After an orientation change or repeated resizing, the main headings, tier prices, FAQ content, and buttons must not overlap one another, and the page must not scroll horizontally. Decorations, stickers, and cards that reappear must use their current grid positions without carrying over stale opacity, scale, or hover displacement.

### R11 - Reduced Motion and Lifecycle
When the user enables a reduced-motion preference and re-enters the page, scroll-revealed content, cards, stickers, and the membership badge must appear directly in their final readable states. The divider strip, hero heading cycle, sticker drift, magnetic displacement, card lift, and custom cursor dot and ring must all stop, while navigation, FAQ expansion, in-page positioning, the email action, and keyboard focus remain usable. After delayed font loading, a window-size change, or a device-orientation change completes, the page must recalculate the hero height and every section's trigger position. Content already revealed must not revert, later sections must enter according to their new positions, and repeated lifecycle updates must not accumulate displacement.
