Acceptance checklist:
## Public acceptance checklist

### R1 - Fixed Navigation and Primary Browsing Entry Points
When the user clicks Adrian JSM, Work, Experience, Skills, Testimonials, or Contact me at the top of the page or from any content section, the page must scroll smoothly within the same document to the hero, work, experience, skills, testimonials, or contact anchor, respectively. Clicking See My Work must instead move to the statistics overview and leave readable space above it. At the top of the page, the navigation must appear as a transparent overlay; after the user scrolls down, it must become a fixed black bar. Narrow viewports must hide the horizontal content links while retaining the brand and contact entry points. Repeated navigation must neither duplicate sections nor clear the contact fields.

### R2 - Hero Copy and Continuous Word Loop
When the user opens the hero section and remains there across multiple transition cycles, the lines of the main heading must enter their layout positions in sequence from a downward offset and transparent state. Ideas, Concepts, Designs, and Code after Shaping, together with their matching icons, must move vertically and continuously in the established order within a fixed-height window. The departing word must be clipped by the window while the next word enters from the adjacent position, and the end of the cycle must roll continuously back to Ideas. Dragging the room, navigating away and then back, or resizing the viewport must not turn this process into a single-frame replacement or reveal multiple rows at once.

### R3 - Hero Room and Progressive Asset Loading
When the user presses and drags on the hero's 3D canvas, the room containing curtains, a desk, a monitor, and a glowing screen must continuously change viewing direction with the pointer and retain its current orientation after release, while the blue-purple lighting and falling white particles continue their own motion. Panning must always remain disabled. Only desktop widths may use the limited zoom range; zoom must be disabled at tablet and narrower widths, and only mobile widths must use the smaller room scale. While the model is loading, the navigation, identity copy, call-to-action entry point, and statistics overview must remain readable. Once ready, the scene must appear within the existing canvas without shifting the hero text or creating a horizontally draggable page area.

### R4 - Project Content and Sequential Entrance
As the user scrolls slowly down from the statistics overview into the Work section, Ryde, The Library Management Platform, and YC Directory - A Startup Showcase App must move upward from lower positions and transition from transparent to visible in the order Ryde, Library, then YC Directory. Each image and title must remain associated with its project, and the Ryde description must remain with Ryde. Once complete, the cards must stay in their final positions. Wide viewports must place the main project in the left column and the two secondary projects in the right column; medium and narrow viewports must rearrange them in content order. Continuing downward, revisiting the section, or entering through the Work anchor must not change their order or associations or create duplicate cards.

### R5 - Statistics, Partner Logos, and Capabilities
When the user clicks See My Work or scrolls into the statistics overview, the values for Years of Experience, Satisfied Clients, Completed Projects, and Client Retention Rate must increase from their initial state to the currently configured values and display the matching suffixes; each value, suffix, and label must remain paired. As the user continues downward, the partner logos must keep moving left in a seamless continuous loop within the faded edges. Quality Focus, Reliable Communication, and On-Time Delivery must retain their respective icons and descriptions and rearrange from a multi-column layout to a single column as the viewport narrows. Re-entering the area must not create overlapping logo strips, and the capability cards must not inherit the pointer-following edge glow used by testimonial cards.

### R6 - Experience Entrance and Timeline Progress
As the user scrolls into Professional Work Experience and reads each experience in sequence, each review-and-company card must fade in from the left, while the role, date, Responsibilities, and company logo must appear in their current experience grouping. As the user scrolls downward, the central black mask must shrink toward the bottom so that the colored line is progressively revealed from the top downward in the reading direction. When the user scrolls back up, the mask must expand and the visible length of the colored line must retreat according to the current position; when scrolling stops, the corresponding progress must remain in place. Both the wide two-sided layout and the narrow vertical layout must preserve the association among each experience's review, company materials, and responsibilities. Repeated scrolling in both directions must not create a second timeline.

### R7 - Skill Card Entrance and 3D Idle Motion
When the user enters How I Can Contribute & My Key Skills, the React Developer, Python Developer, Backend Developer, Interactive Developer, and Project Manager cards must fade in sequentially from below. Each 3D logo must continue to float gently and change orientation within its own canvas, independently of the others. Dragging must change only the current logo's viewing direction, and the mouse wheel must not change its zoom. When the pointer enters a card, the blue-gray background must fill upward from the bottom; when the pointer leaves, it must retreat to the bottom. After a viewport-width change, the cards must rearrange from a horizontal circular array into fewer columns or a single column of rounded cards, while each name, viewing direction, and idle state remains isolated from adjacent cards.

### R8 - Testimonial Content and Edge Glow
When the user scrolls to What People Say About Me? and moves the pointer into any client testimonial card, that card's localized edge glow must appear, and its angle must update continuously with the pointer's position relative to the center of the card, while the star graphic, testimonial text, avatar, name, and account handle remain in place. The glow must fade out after the pointer leaves. If the user moves quickly from one testimonial card to another, only the current card must respond to the new pointer position, and the previous card must retain no visible glow. After rearrangement among multiple columns on wide viewports, fewer columns at medium widths, and one column on narrow viewports, every testimonial, regardless of length, must retain its complete person information.

### R9 - Contact Form Submission States
As the user fills in Your name, Your Email, and Your Message within Get in Touch – Let’s Connect, each of the three fields must independently display its latest input. The form must prevent submission when required content is missing or the email does not satisfy the email input constraint. After valid content is submitted, the button must change from Send Message to Sending.... On successful submission, all three fields must be cleared and the button must return to Send Message. If submission is unsuccessful, the entered content must be retained and the sending state must end. While the user scrolls away, returns through an anchor, or resizes the viewport, entrance behavior in other sections must not overwrite the field values.

### R10 - Contact Scene and Footer Information
When the user drags on the warm-orange computer desk canvas on the right side of the contact section, the viewing direction must change continuously within the limited vertical-angle range, while mouse-wheel zoom remains disabled. Interacting with the scene must not alter the name, email, message, or sending state on the left. On wide viewports, the form and scene must occupy separate columns; on narrow viewports, the scene must follow the form and retain its own usable interaction height. Continuing to the footer must reveal Terms & Conditions, the current set of social icons, and the Adrian Hajdin copyright line generated with the current local year. After a viewport change, these items must retain their existing content order, and graphic containers without navigation destinations must not be expanded into new page entry points.

### R11 - Responsive Behavior and Session Lifecycle
When the user switches among wide, medium, and narrow viewports while in the hero, work, experience, skills, or contact section, the room scale and zoom availability, project columns, experience timeline position, skill column count, testimonial column count, and contact-section stacking order must adapt to their respective containers. The page must not develop horizontal blank space, the fixed navigation must remain within the viewport, and content order must remain unchanged. If models, images, or fonts become ready later, existing text and the form must remain usable. Within the same session, anchor navigation and size changes must preserve entered values and must not duplicate content; after a reload, exactly one current content set and one entrance state must be established.

### R12 - Stable Room Orientation During Work Scrolling
When the user scrolls slowly from the Hero into Work, views the Ryde, Library, and YC Directory cards in sequence, or immediately reverses back toward the room canvas, the cards may reveal according to their own entrance progress but must not drive the 3D room or monitor orientation. Reversing the scroll must not leave residual yaw from the previous direction, and stopping must leave the room and monitor continuously at their intended orientation rather than waiting for card animation progress to catch up.
