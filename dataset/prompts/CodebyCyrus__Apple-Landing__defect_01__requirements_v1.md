Acceptance checklist:
## Public acceptance checklist

### R1 - Header and Responsive Hero
When users open the page on wide and narrow screens and wait for the hero section to appear, they must see the Apple logo, product title, muted inline hero media, Buy button, and the "From $199/month or $999" price information. The wide layout must show Store, Mac, iPhone, and Support with landscape media; the narrow layout must hide the centered navigation labels and switch to portrait media while retaining the search and shopping bag icons on the right. When the user clicks Buy, the page must move to the "Get the highlights." section and bring that section's heading, first media card, and control group into the current viewport. The URL fragment and scroll destination must remain synchronized, and returning to the hero section and clicking Buy again must lead to the same content entry point.

### R2 - Automatic Highlight Sequence Advancement
When the user scrolls into "Get the highlights." and waits for playback, the first A17 Pro card must play first, and the active progress dot must expand and fill according to the video's current time. As each segment ends, the track must move continuously and horizontally to the corresponding Titanium, telephoto-capability, and Action button cards, with each title overlay traveling with its own media. Only the current card may be playing at any moment; all other videos must remain at either their not-yet-started or completed positions. The track offset, active card position, progress color, and control icon must update in sync, and the track must not move into a blank area after the final card ends.

### R3 - Playback Controls and Progress Synchronization
When the user clicks pause while a highlight video is playing, the current frame, media time, and progress fill must all freeze together; clicking play must resume from that same position. During repeated clicks on the control, the play and pause icons must always correspond one-to-one with the actual playback state, and no other card may start. After the user waits for the final video to finish and clicks replay, the track must return to the first card, clear the final-segment completion state, and restart the first card's progress before continuing in the established order. Repeated replays must not stack timing updates or create multiple active progress bars.

### R4 - 3D Loading and Drag-to-Inspect
When the user scrolls to "Take a Special Look.", the viewing area must first show "Loading..." if the model is not yet ready. Once loading finishes, the message must be replaced by a lit iPhone model. While the user holds the model and drags horizontally or diagonally, the phone must rotate continuously around its center and, upon release, remain at the resulting side or oblique angle. Dragging must not change the page's scroll position, the model size, or the current titanium finish; zooming and panning must remain disabled. When the user leaves the section and returns, the 3D display must still be confined to the viewing container, without its WebGL rendering covering the heading, finish description, or control group.

### R5 - Titanium Finish Selection
As the user clicks the swatches for Natural Titanium, Blue Titanium, White Titanium, and Black Titanium in turn, the active model's configurable body material and screen texture must switch to the selected finish. The title below the model must update in sync to the corresponding full label: "iPhone 15 Pro in Natural Titanium", "iPhone 15 Pro in Blue Titanium", "iPhone 15 Pro in White Titanium", or "iPhone 15 Pro in Black Titanium". The change must preserve the current size and the viewing orientation already set by the user, without showing "Loading..." again. If the user switches between the two sizes and then selects another finish, the same finish state must apply to the newly active model and persist when switching back and forth. Repeatedly clicking the already selected swatch must not create duplicate models, change the highlighted size button, or clear the dragged orientation.

### R6 - Dual-Size Switching and Orientation Memory
After the user drags the 6.1" model to a clear side view, selects 6.7", and drags the larger model to a different oblique angle, switching back and forth between the two size buttons must animate the two views horizontally out and in. The active button must simultaneously change to a white background with black text, and each size must restore the non-default viewing orientation saved when the user last released that model. Returning to either 6.1" or 6.7" must not automatically restore the front view, and the finish title must remain consistent with the selected finish. Clicking the currently selected size again must not start an additional transition or reset the viewing controls, and rapid back-and-forth switching must still leave only one operable view visible.

### R7 - Scrolling Through the Titanium Story
As the user scrolls downward from "Explore the full story.", the page must first show "iPhone. Forged in Titanium." with the muted product video. Next, the camera close-up and side-button close-up must progressively settle from an enlarged, transparent state to normal scale and full visibility according to scroll progress, followed by the two titanium descriptions moving upward from below into their final positions. If the user stops within the image area, scrolls backward, and then moves forward again, both images' scale and opacity must correspond directly to the current scroll position and remain synchronized with each other. Once the descriptions have finished appearing, they must remain readable, and the video, images, and copy must continue to align to the same content width.

### R8 - A17 Pro Gaming Showcase
When the user continues scrolling into the A17 Pro section, the chip graphic must shrink back and appear from an enlarged, transparent state. The heading "A17 Pro chip. A monster win for gaming.", the landscape phone frame, and the Honkai: Star Rail video must then appear in content order; once the media has loaded, it must play muted and inline inside the frame. When the user reaches the performance descriptions, the graphics-performance copy, immersive-gaming description, and "New / Pro-class GPU / with 6 cores" must remain one semantic group. On narrow screens, the left and right columns must stack vertically, and the video must not extend beyond the phone frame or cover the heading.

### R9 - Section Reveals and Reverse Scrolling
As the user scrolls continuously from the hero section through the highlights, 3D, titanium, and chip sections, each section heading and description must move upward from a lower, low-opacity state into its final position, while progress-bound images change continuously with the scroll position. Completed model selections and carousel state must not be recreated by these text reveals. If the user reverse-scrolls past a text reveal point governed by reversible behavior and then enters it again, that object must exit and reappear along the same path. Objects with one-time reveals, such as the chip graphic, must remain complete after appearing and must not repeatedly flash within the current viewport without a new reveal event.

### R10 - Viewport Changes and Layout Reflow
When the user resizes the browser from a wide viewport to a narrow one and then restores it, the hero section must switch between portrait and landscape product media, while the header navigation, highlight cards, titanium images and copy, A17 Pro two-column content, and footer reflow for the current width. The main page must not allow horizontal content scrolling; only the highlights section may clip its own horizontally moving track. After the size change, the 3D canvas must update its display dimensions with the browser viewport, the model must remain within the viewing area, and the color swatches and 6.1" and 6.7" buttons must remain operable. The current finish, current size, and button highlight must stay synchronized, and the newly selected hero media must use the resource suited to the current composition rather than retaining the previous width's aspect ratio.

### R11 - Loading Lifecycle and Footer Completion
When the user opens or refreshes the page before its resources are ready, the text structure, media containers, highlight-control positions, and 3D "Loading..." message must be established first. A single current highlight segment may begin only after all highlight videos are ready, and once the model is ready, the page must only remove the loading message and enable dragging. Repeated refreshes must not multiply the control groups, progress updates, or pointer responses. At the bottom of the page, the user must see Go To Main Website, LinkedIn, the phone number, copyright information, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Sales Policy, Legal, and Site Map. On narrow screens, this content may wrap but must remain within the footer boundaries. After the user returns to content above, the media, model, and selection state must continue to operate according to their lifecycle rules.
