Acceptance checklist:
## Public acceptance checklist

### R1 - Fixed Navigation and Anchor State
When the user clicks Home, About Us, Our Service, and Testimonials in sequence in the fixed desktop navigation bar, the page scrolls smoothly to each corresponding section anchor and shows the item clicked on that occasion in blue text with a full underline. The top bar remains above the page content and leaves enough clearance above the destination heading. When the user opens the menu on a narrow screen and selects from the same set of links, the page likewise reaches the destination section, the selected item becomes the current item in blue text, and the expanded panel closes as part of that selection. The current selection remains selected when the menu is opened again.

### R2 - Hero Growth Conversion Entry Point
When the user opens the page, the Home section reveals, in layered order, the growth badge, the main heading containing Startup to Fortune 500, the business description, the email input, and the blue arrow button. On desktop, the team meeting image appears on the right; on a narrow screen, the image follows the text. When the user focuses the email field and types characters, hovers over the image, or presses the action button, the field shows its focus treatment and retains the entered text, the image enlarges slightly, and the button changes color and shadow, scales down when pressed, and then returns to its resting state.

### R3 - Continuous Partner Logos and Team Principles
After the user scrolls past the Hero, the Proud partner at Hubspot & Segment message stays on the left side of the partner-logo strip while the Slack, Amazon, WooCommerce, MeUndies, and SitePoint images move continuously at a constant speed in a duplicated horizontal sequence, with uninterrupted occupancy where the sequences join. When the user hovers over any logo, only that logo changes from grayscale and low opacity back to full color. The user then reaches the About Us section and sees the symbols, titles, and descriptions for Built for impact and In sync with you in reading order.

### R4 - Service Workflow and Content Reveal
As the user continues scrolling into the How can we help your business? section, the three workflow items Find out what you need, Work out the details, and We get to work fast enter their final positions in sequence, each with its own icon background color, title, and description. The Become a Partner button appears below the workflow. On desktop, the three items are evenly spaced in a horizontal row; on mobile, they form a vertical column in reading order. When the user hovers over and presses the button, it enlarges and rebounds, while the workflow cards and heading retain their original layout space.

### R5 - Scheduling and Monitoring Narrative
When the user scrolls to the SCHEDULE and MONITOR sections, the statistics dashboard image and scheduling copy first appear in left and right columns, followed by the monitoring copy and mobile dashboard image in the opposite text-and-image order. Both text links retain their arrows and their respective labels. As each section enters the viewport, its image, heading, body copy, and link complete their reveal from transparent, offset states. When the viewport narrows, the columns stack in content order, each image keeps its aspect ratio and scales within its container, and the text does not overlap the media. After the reflow, each link arrow and label remain together on the same clickable row and preserve the original reading order.

### R6 - Product Count and Plan Pricing
When the user sets the product slider in the Pricing section to any integer n from 1 through 50, the product-count display reads n products, Starter shows the nearest-integer monthly price calculated as 4,000 × n ÷ 50, and Business shows the nearest-integer monthly price calculated as 7,500 × n ÷ 50. Both cards retain the per-month unit. Whether the user drags continuously, clicks the track, releases the pointer, or uses the keyboard to increase or decrease the value, the current slider value, product-count display, and both plan prices all refresh using the same n from that input. Setting the same value repeatedly leaves the result unchanged, and returning from either endpoint to an intermediate value follows the same calculation and display relationship.

### R7 - Service Card Response
After the user enters the Our Service section, the left side shows Future of support with new shape, the two capability points, and Get started, while the right side shows four service cards—Web Design, Ad-Creatives, Automation, and Infographics—each with a colored icon, description, and LEARN MORE link. As the user hovers over the cards one by one, only the current card enlarges and gains a stronger shadow, then returns to its original size when the pointer leaves. On a narrow screen, the cards appear in a single column in reading order, and the links and button remain focusable and pressable.

### R8 - Testimonial Carousel Record Consistency
When the user clicks the right or left arrow in the Testimonials section, the testimonial track moves smoothly in the corresponding direction to the adjacent record. Throughout the movement, every card continues to contain the circular profile photo, five stars, name, and review text belonging to the same customer; once the transition finishes, the new group stops in the visible area. Mobile, medium, and wide viewports show one, two, and three cards respectively. After a size change, the carousel recalculates the visible width while preserving record order. An arrow at a boundary where no further movement is available becomes less opaque and stops responding.

### R9 - Newsletter and Footer Information
After the user reaches the Subscribe newsletter section through the desktop Get in touch action, the user can type in the Enter your email address field and see hover and press feedback on the green Discover button. On mobile, the desktop layout with a diagonally divided two-tone background and a side-by-side form changes to a vertical, full-width arrangement. As the user continues to the footer, the brand description, four link groups, and three social links all appear. Hovering produces the corresponding color change or positional shift. The year at the bottom matches the current calendar year at the time of the visit and appears beside the author information, or stacks above or below it on a narrow screen.

### R10 - Responsive Layout and Page Lifecycle
When the user resizes the browser among desktop, tablet, and mobile widths, the Hero, scheduling, monitoring, pricing, services, testimonials, and newsletter sections reflow at their respective breakpoints. Images retain their proportions, body content does not extend beyond the viewport, the fixed navigation changes to menu mode, and the page does not develop horizontal scrolling. After the page is reloaded, Home returns to the selected state, the mobile menu is closed, the product count returns to 1, and the testimonials start from the first record. Each section completes its offset-and-opacity reveal when it enters the viewport, and completed animations do not change the final layout dimensions.
